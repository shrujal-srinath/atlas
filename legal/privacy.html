ATLAS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

ATLAS ("the app", "we") is a personal training, nutrition, and productivity tracker. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your rights over it. Questions: shrujalsrinath@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account — your email address and display name, used to sign you in and identify your account. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your email, name, and Google account ID from Google.
  • Activity you log — habits, tasks, completion records, food and water entries, body weight and measurements, mood and journal entries, focus sessions, goals, and notes.
  • Photos — if you enable photo proof for a habit, the captured photo is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Diagnostics — if the app crashes, a non-personal error message and stack trace may be sent so we can fix bugs.

2. How your data is stored and protected

Your data is stored in our backend provider, Supabase (PostgreSQL), and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Row-Level Security ensures each account can only ever read or write its own data. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

3. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your rights

  • Export — you can export your data as CSV from Settings.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings; this cannot be undone.
  • Access & correction — you can view and edit all your data within the app.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — only when you take a habit-proof photo (stored locally).
  • Notifications & alarms — to deliver reminders you schedule.

6. Children

ATLAS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
