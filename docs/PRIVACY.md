# ATLAS — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 June 2026_

ATLAS ("the app", "we") is a personal-performance tracker. This policy explains
what data the app collects, why, and your choices. Host this document at a
public URL and link it from the app and your store listings.

> **Action before publishing:** replace `[CONTACT EMAIL]` and `[HOSTED URL]`
> below, and update `_privacyPolicyUrl` / `_termsUrl` in
> `lib/features/settings/screens/settings_screen.dart` to the hosted URLs.

## What we collect
You create the data; we store it so the app works across sessions and devices.

- **Account**: email address and display name (for sign-in, via our auth provider).
- **Activity you log**: habits and completions, tasks/to-dos, focus sessions.
- **Health & fitness**: food/nutrition entries, water intake, body weight,
  body measurements, mood/energy check-ins, and journal entries.
- **Notes** you create.
- **Notification preferences** and locally scheduled reminder settings.
- **Diagnostics**: if the app encounters an error, a crash report (error
  message, stack trace, platform, and your user id) is sent so we can fix bugs.

We do **not** sell your data, show third-party ads, or use advertising/tracking
SDKs.

## Device permissions
- **Camera** — only when you scan a barcode or attach a photo; images/scans are
  used for that action and not collected for any other purpose.
- **Notifications** — to deliver the reminders you enable.

## How data is used
Solely to provide the app's features: showing your history, scores, trends, and
reminders, and syncing your data to your account. Crash diagnostics are used
only to diagnose and fix defects.

## Where it's stored & who processes it
- Your data is stored in our backend database hosted by **Supabase** (our data
  processor), protected by row-level security so each account can only access
  its own data.
- **Open Food Facts** is queried for barcode/branded-food lookups. These are
  product queries; your personal data is not sent to them.

## Retention & deletion
Your data is retained while your account exists. You can **delete your account**
in-app (Settings → Account → Delete account), which removes your account and
your associated data. You can also sign out at any time.

## Children
ATLAS is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region).

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes
will be surfaced in-app where appropriate.
